# Code audit, 10 October 2026 (second pass)

Where the app stands after Round 17, what the first audit left, and what
this pass found. Numbers are from the tree as of this date.

## Size and shape

- App and server: ~31,200 lines of TypeScript/TSX (was ~27,000), tests
  ~8,000 lines: 348 unit tests (Vitest, ~6 s) and 19 end-to-end tests
  (Playwright; 29 runs pass, 9 skip on the phone project, see below).
- 52 runtime and 21 dev dependencies, all in use.
- Built: 130 MB on disk, 116 MB of it map data served on demand. JavaScript
  as before: the ArcGIS projection engine 1.19 MB, `context` 443 kB,
  `thermometer` 404 kB (references and voronoi), `Map` 231 kB.
- Largest files: `gameOps.ts` 1,403 (was 684), `CardsPanel.tsx` 870,
  `sidebar-l.tsx` 828 (shadcn), `GamePanel.tsx` 675, `measuring.ts` 651,
  `session.ts` 609, `overpass.ts` 573, `GameSetupDialog.tsx` 548.
- `any` appears 127 times (was 162), mostly in PolygonDraw, the cards,
  overpass and references; the lint rule for it is off. No TODOs.

## Fixed while auditing (regressions from Round 17)

- Duplicate merging compared every place with every other: thousands of
  parks would have frozen the phone for seconds. Places are now compared
  by name only, and rejection lookups are indexed.
- With teams, the next hider could only be claimed after a stopped clock,
  so a round that never got going (no clock) could not be handed over.
  Any clock that isn't running or paused now allows it.

## Found in this pass

### Bugs and risks

Points 1 to 7 below were fixed the same day (plan, round 17, item 34);
8 and 9 stay open.

1. **Stale delete requests.** A Move archives every question but leaves
   `deleteRequests`; the orphaned keys would read "A question" in events.
   A new round clears them; a Move should too. (Small.)
2. **Two hiders.** The tentative station pick lives on one phone; the
   partner's phone sees only the confirmed station. Fine when they hide
   together, surprising when they split up to scout.
3. **Rejections and the nearest-place maps.** A kind with rejections
   bypasses the pre-computed cells and builds a voronoi diagram from the
   full place list on the phone. For parks or museums in a whole canton
   that is thousands of points: slow, maybe too slow. Either cap it with
   a note, or rebuild the cells on the server from the rejections.
4. **Dead code behind the hidden types.** The "hiding zones only" place
   questions are off for good but still in the schema, in
   `hidingZoneLayer.applyQuestionToHidingZones` and in `questionInfo`;
   the reference page may still list them. Remove once no saved question
   can carry them.
5. **Unnamed places.** Merging and rejecting go by name, so unnamed
   places are neither merged nor rejectable; the questions already ignore
   unnamed places for "same nearest X", so this only shows in the review.
6. **The deck log** goes to the seekers ("Ben drew 3 extra cards, keeping
   1"); open question from Round 17.
7. **Previews linger.** The hider's answer previews are cleared on
   joining a game, not on leaving one. Cosmetic.
8. **Phone viewports untested for games.** 9 of the 19 end-to-end tests
   are desktop-only, among them every shared-game flow, so the sheet
   layout of everything new (the team picker, the next-hider popup, the
   found popup, the place review, the answer list) has never run in a
   phone-sized browser.
9. **Full-state broadcast** (first audit, point 4) matters more now: each
   round's archive keeps full question entries in `rounds` and `moves`.

### Structure

- `gameOps.ts` wants splitting by feature: questions and answers; cards
  and the deck; curses; the clock, rounds and teams; the hider's station
  and pin. The pure-function shape makes that mechanical.
- `CardsPanel.tsx` still mixes the hand, the draw flows, the chooser
  dialogs, the history and the curses; the Duplicate and casting-cost
  flows added state to it.
- Small helpers are copied around: `ago()` three times, `time()` twice,
  the home-game place list in five files. One `lib/format.ts` and one
  `PLACE_TYPES` (now in `settings.ts`) would do.
- `context.ts` holds 58 stores; `session.ts` 26 exports. Both are the
  first thing anything imports, so they stay large; a split by feature
  (play area, questions, settings, map view) is still the right move.

### Tests

- The rules are well covered. Library modules without a unit test:
  `reference.ts` (needs map data), `hiderSpot.ts`, `knownGames.ts`,
  `alerts.ts`, `gameSettings.ts`, `photos.ts`, `deckSession.ts`.
- The end-to-end suite covers the whole round from setup to the next
  hider on desktop; the phone project gap is point 8 above.

## Suggested order

1. A shakedown game with teams and the new curses; fix what it finds.
2. Run the shared-game end-to-end tests on the phone project too (the
   sheet; most of them only need the sidebar opened differently).
3. Split `gameOps.ts` and `CardsPanel.tsx`; share the small helpers.
4. The small fixes: a Move clears delete requests, previews clear on
   leaving, the reference page and dead code behind the hidden types.
5. The rejection fallback (point 3) before anyone rejects a park kind in a
   big area.
6. Live view without the archive; then as before: Authelia, the bundle,
   the worker, the offline map.
